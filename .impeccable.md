# .impeccable.md — Pioneers United Digital Profiles

## Design Context

### Users
Two distinct populations, both must be designed for:

- **Executives (profile owners)**: senior operators at Pioneers United whose Digital
  Profile is printed on business cards and shared over WhatsApp/LinkedIn. They never see
  the public page as "their app" — they experience it through the admin dashboard when
  editing.
- **Visitors (profile viewers)** — the primary audience for the public profile page:
  predominantly **business contacts — prospective clients, partners, vendors** meeting
  the executive for the first time via a scanned QR code or a shared link, on a phone, in
  a business context (a meeting, a conference, right after an introduction). They are
  deciding, in seconds, whether this person and organization are credible. Secondary: the
  executive re-sharing or checking their own profile.

### Brand Personality
**Trust / competence.** Not prestige-for-its-own-sake, not warmth-first. The interface
should feel like well-made enterprise software: precise, dependable, understated —
confidence that doesn't need to perform. The existing design system already encodes this
directionally — navy authority, a bronze accent used only as a restrained signature (never
a fill, never a gradient), architectural 8px-max radii, hairline shadows over glow. Any new
UI work should reinforce competence and clarity over ornament.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, paper-based, editorial-architectural. Archivo (Latin) + Tajawal (Arabic),
IBM Plex Mono for slugs/IDs/phone numbers. Motion is restrained to fade + 4px rise; nothing
scales, bounces, or slides decoratively.

**Anti-references — explicitly avoid:**
- **Generic SaaS landing page**: hero-metric layouts (big number + small label + gradient
  accent), identical icon-topped card grids, pitch-deck visual rhythm. A Digital Profile is
  a *credential document*, not a product marketing page — it should not read like one.
- Secondary, already covered by the existing system but worth restating: the generic
  AI/dark-mode aesthetic (cyan-on-dark, glassmorphism, gradient text, neon glow) — the
  system's light paper base and hairline elevation already rule this out structurally.

### Design Principles
1. **Credential, not pitch.** Every section on the public profile should read like part of
   a printed dossier a business contact would trust on sight — not a conversion funnel.
   No stat tiles, no "hero metric" framing, no marketing card grids.
2. **Bronze is a signature, never a device.** The copper accent marks one or two
   deliberate moments (the rule, the QR corner ticks, a badge) — reaching for it more
   often than the design system already does dilutes it into decoration.
3. **Mobile-first, meeting-context legibility.** The visitor is very often reading this on
   a phone screen within seconds of meeting the executive — hierarchy must resolve at a
   glance, touch targets must be unambiguous, and nothing should require scrolling to find
   the one thing (contact action) a visitor came for.
4. **Bilingual parity is structural, not a translation pass.** Arabic is not a mirrored
   afterthought — RTL layout, the Arabic type ramp, and the absence of Latin micro-cap
   styling under `dir="rtl"` all have to hold up as first-class, not "close enough."
5. **Restraint reads as competence.** When in doubt, remove ornament rather than add it.
   The hairline-elevation-over-glow, no-gradient, max-8px-radius stance is deliberate; a
   fix that reaches for a shadow, a gradient, or a rounder corner to "make it feel nicer"
   is working against the system, not with it.
